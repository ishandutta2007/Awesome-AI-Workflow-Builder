# Awesome-AI-Workflow-Builder

## Top AI Workflow Builder Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Visual AI Orchestration, LLM Flow Builders, Agentic Workflows, No-Code Automation & Production AI Pipelines*  
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **AI Workflow Builders**. These tools let teams design multi-step LLM and agent workflows on a visual canvas—connecting models, tools, RAG, triggers, and APIs—then deploy them as APIs or automations without heavy custom code.

**Examples** include Flowise, Dify, Langflow, n8n AI, Zapier AI, Relay.app, Pipedream AI, BuildShip, Activepieces AI, and Stack AI (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This category is dominated by open source. **Langflow**, **Flowise**, **Dify**, **n8n**, and **Activepieces** are MIT/Apache-licensed and power most self-hosted AI workflow stacks. This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Dify Cloud](https://dify.ai/)**  
  Hosted production platform for agentic workflows, RAG, and AI apps—visual canvas with knowledge bases, citations, and deployment (open-source core available).

- **[Flowise Cloud](https://flowiseai.com/)**  
  Hosted visual LangChain/agent builder—drag-and-drop nodes for LLM chains, tools, and agents with API export.

- **[Langflow (hosted options)](https://www.langflow.org/)**  
  Visual Python-backed builder for agents, RAG, and MCP servers; self-host primary, with managed options available.

- **[n8n Cloud](https://n8n.io/)**  
  Hosted workflow automation with strong AI nodes—general automation plus LLM/agent steps; fair-code open core.

- **[Zapier AI, Pipedream AI, Relay.app](https://zapier.com/ai)**  
  Mainstream automation platforms with AI steps, agents, and natural-language workflow building for business users.

- **[BuildShip, Stack AI, Activepieces Cloud](https://buildship.com/)**  
  Low-code AI workflow and backend builders for APIs, agents, and automations with hosted runtimes.

- **[Other commercial AI workflow platforms](https://dify.ai/)**  
  Additional visual orchestrators and AI automation products for teams and enterprises.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Langflow](https://github.com/langflow-ai/langflow)**  
  Leading open-source (MIT) visual builder for AI agents, RAG apps, and MCP servers—Python-backed, drag-and-drop canvas, highly extensible.

- **[Flowise](https://github.com/FlowiseAI/Flowise)**  
  Open-source (Apache 2.0) drag-and-drop UI for LangChain LLM workflows and agents—300+ nodes, self-hosted, rapid prototyping.

- **[Dify](https://github.com/langgenius/dify)**  
  Open-source production platform for agentic workflows and RAG—visual canvas, knowledge bases, hybrid search, and citation tracking built in.

- **[n8n](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n)**  
  Fair-code open workflow automation platform with extensive integrations and AI/LLM nodes—self-host for full control of business + AI automations.

- **[Activepieces](https://github.com/activepieces/activepieces)**  
  Open-source (MIT) no-code automation alternative to n8n/Zapier—visual flows, AI pieces, unlimited self-hosted runs.

- **[Windmill](https://github.com/windmill-labs/windmill)**  
  Open-source developer-oriented workflow and internal-tool platform—scripts, flows, AI agent steps, and MCP support.

- **[Botpress (open), Stack-style open builders](https://github.com/botpress/botpress)**  
  Open conversational and workflow platforms that support AI agent and automation patterns.

- **[Node-RED & general visual automation](https://github.com/node-red/node-red)**  
  Mature open visual programming tool sometimes extended with LLM nodes for IoT and automation workflows.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **AI-first visual builders**: Langflow, Flowise, and Dify for agents and RAG pipelines.
- **General automation + AI**: n8n and Activepieces when workflows span SaaS apps and LLMs.
- **Code-first flows**: Windmill for engineers who want scripts and AI steps in one place.
- **Composable stacks**: Open builder → self-hosted models/tools → API or webhook triggers.
- Commercial platforms still lead in polished consumer UX, managed scale, and non-technical team adoption.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
**Langflow**, **Flowise**, and **Dify** are the core open AI workflow builders.  
**n8n** and **Activepieces** excel when AI is one step in broader automation.  
Commercial clouds (Dify Cloud, Flowise Cloud, n8n Cloud, Zapier AI, Pipedream, BuildShip, Stack AI, etc.) provide zero-ops hosting and team features.  
Most technical teams self-host Langflow/Flowise/Dify/n8n and optionally use commercial builders for business users. Fully open stacks are production-ready with your own infrastructure.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- AI workflows can call external APIs, modify data, and trigger side effects. Use least-privilege credentials, approval steps for high-impact actions, and thorough testing before production.
- Open-source builders offer full control and no per-run fees when self-hosted but require you to secure, update, and scale the stack. Commercial platforms shift operational burden to the vendor. Choose based on technical capacity and compliance needs.
